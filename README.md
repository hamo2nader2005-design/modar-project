# Modar | مُدار

Modar is a B2B SaaS platform designed to help small and medium businesses manage customer communication, sales, bookings, and team performance from one place.

## Overview

Modar brings multiple customer communication channels into one unified system, helping businesses manage conversations, employees, bookings, and sales more efficiently.

## Key Features

- Unified inbox for WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, and Telegram
- Automatic conversation distribution among team members
- AI-powered replies for frequently asked questions
- Customer database management
- Employee performance tracking
- Sales and customer service queues
- Marketing campaigns and bulk messaging
- Booking and sales website builder
- Reports for response time, inactive time, and resolved conversations

## Target Market

The platform primarily targets small and medium businesses in Saudi Arabia, especially:

- Clinics and medical centers
- Salons and beauty centers
- Educational and training centers
- Businesses that depend heavily on WhatsApp for customer communication

## Business Model

- B2B SaaS
- Monthly subscription model
- Designed for scalable business growth
